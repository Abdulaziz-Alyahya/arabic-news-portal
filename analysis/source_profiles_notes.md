# Source-Level Orientation Notes

## Al Jazeera

Political alignment: unclear

Ideological tendency: unclear

Confidence: low

Basis:
Al Jazeera describes itself as an independent news organization that is partly funded by the Qatari government. Government funding alone is not enough to assign a specific political or ideological orientation, so I kept the classification unclear for this version.

Source:
Al Jazeera - About Us


## DW Arabic

Political alignment: independent

Ideological tendency: unclear

Confidence: medium

Basis:
DW is publicly funded through the German federal budget, but its editorial independence is protected by the Deutsche Welle Act. Because of this, I classified its political alignment as independent. I did not assign a specific ideological tendency because there was not enough evidence for a clear label in this version.

Sources:
Deutsche Welle Act
DW Sustainability Report 2022


## Important Note

These labels describe source-level political or ideological orientation only. They do not measure truthfulness, factuality, or journalistic quality.

When there is not enough evidence for a clear classification, I use "unclear" instead of forcing a label.
